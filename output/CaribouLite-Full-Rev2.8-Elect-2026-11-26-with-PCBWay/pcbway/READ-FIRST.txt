PCBWay returned fabrication data — read first

This revised package adds PCBWay's returned CAM files to the original design
reference package. Start with pcbway/EQ3/CAM/ when inspecting the supplier's
panelized fabrication geometry. The original supplier ZIP is included unchanged.
The EQ3 engineering-question workbook and CA45470C04A0 stack-up/impedance PDF
are also included unchanged. These were recovered directly from the local
manufacturing-and-working-files backup archive.

The local 24 September EQ3 review records describe:
- L1-L2 and L3-L4 blind-via laser holes changed to 0.127 mm.
- One GND stitching via near U19/C40/J4 shifted +0.0334 mm in X, repeated
  in all six panel copies; affected copper and drill features moved together.
- Revised Shengyi S1000H/S1000HB stack-up, Er1 4.09, 106+106 outer prepreg,
  nominal finished thickness 1.60 mm +/-0.16 mm.
- Calculated impedances: L1/L4 exposed RF 48.62/48.46 ohms; covered RF
  47.14/46.96 ohms; L4 differential 91.81 ohms. See supplier report for
  construction, models and production tolerances.
- EQ3's 14 CAM files are recorded as identical to the preceding EQ2 CAM files.
